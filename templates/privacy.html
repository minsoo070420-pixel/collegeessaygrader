How We Handle Your Essay

We don't store it

There is no database in this app. Once your results page loads, your essay isn't kept anywhere on the server — it exists only in memory for the few seconds it takes to process your request, then it's gone.

We don't log it

Server logs never record what you actually submitted — only technical details like whether a request succeeded, never your essay's content.

It is sent to Google's Gemini AI

To generate your feedback, your essay is sent to Google's Gemini API, which is what actually reads and grades it — this is required for the AI feedback feature to work at all. This app currently runs on Gemini's free tier, and under Google's own policy, content submitted on the free tier may be used by Google to improve their models. Keep that in mind before submitting anything you'd want to keep fully private.

My personal commitment to you

Beyond what's required to generate your feedback, I will never read, share, sell, or otherwise use your essay for any purpose. This site was built to give students honest, useful feedback — not to collect or exploit anyone's personal writing.
